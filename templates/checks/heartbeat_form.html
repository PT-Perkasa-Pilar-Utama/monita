How a heartbeat works

  1. After you save, Monita gives the heartbeat a secret ping URL.
  2. Your job calls that URL when it finishes. Each call moves the deadline to the time of the call plus the period and the grace time.
  3. If no call arrives by the deadline, Monita opens an incident. The next call closes it.
  4. A call to the URL with /fail at the end opens an incident at once.

The first deadline is the period plus the grace time from the moment you save.
